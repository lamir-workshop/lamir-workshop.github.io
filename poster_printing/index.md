# Poster Printing

Thanks to the generous support of our [sponsors]({{ '/sponsors/' | relative_url }}), LAMIR 2026 offers **free poster printing** for workshop presenters.

Prepare your poster in **portrait orientation, 90 cm wide × 120 cm tall**. We’ll print it for the poster session and provide the rods and tripods for display.

**Submission deadline: Tuesday, November 24, 2026.** To request printing, submit your poster using the form below.
