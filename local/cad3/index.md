LAMIR 2026 will be held at **Centro de Atividades Didáticas 3 (CAD3)**, on the **Pampulha campus of the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG)** in Belo Horizonte.

CAD3 is a teaching facility with classrooms and auditoriums, serving activities across the university.
