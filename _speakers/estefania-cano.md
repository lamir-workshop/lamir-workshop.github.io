[Estefanía Cano](https://ecanoc.github.io/) is Chief Scientist at AudioSourceRE, where she develops machine learning tools for separating sounds in audio recordings. At Fraunhofer IDMT in Germany, she worked on music information retrieval, source separation, and audio fingerprinting. At A\*STAR in Singapore, she developed medical technologies that use music.
